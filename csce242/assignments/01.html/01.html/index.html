The History of Basketball

By Dakarai Mcclam

Basketball

Basketball is a sport that was invented in 1891 by Dr. James Naismith. It is played by two teams of five players each, who try to score points by throwing a ball through a hoop. The game has evolved over the years and is now played professionally in many countries around the world.

Basketball has become one of the most popular sports in the world, with millions of fans and players. It is known for its fast-paced action, exciting plays, and incredible athleticism.
